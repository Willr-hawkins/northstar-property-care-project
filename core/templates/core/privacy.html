Privacy

Privacy notice

This is a portfolio demo. Northstar Property Care is a fictional company, and this notice describes how this demo site handles information. It isn't legal advice. Please don't submit real personal details.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through the quote form, we collect:

  • your name and email address
  • your phone number, if you choose to give it
  • your postcode, so we know whether you're in our area
  • the service you're interested in and a description of your project
  • how you'd prefer us to contact you, if you tell us

We don't ask for anything else, and we don't need it to reply.

Why we collect it

Only to reply to your enquiry and, if you'd like to go ahead, to arrange a visit and prepare a quote. We don't use your details for marketing, and we never sell or share them.

Who can see it

Your enquiry is stored securely and can only be seen by Northstar staff who have signed in. When you send an enquiry, a notification email is sent to our team through our email provider, SendGrid.

How long we keep it

On a real site, enquiries that don't go ahead would be deleted after a set period. On this demo, sample data is reset regularly.

Cookies

This site only uses cookies that are essential for it to work securely: a security cookie that protects forms from misuse, and a sign-in cookie for staff. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. On a real site, you would contact the business directly to do this.
