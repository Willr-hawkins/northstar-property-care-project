{# ---------- Story ---------- #}

Why we work the way we do.

Northstar started with a simple frustration: too many renovations go wrong in the gaps between trades. The plasterer blames the electrician, the quote doesn't match the bill, and nobody is quite sure who's in charge.

So we built a team that covers the whole job, from the first measured visit through design, building and finishing, and that stays around afterwards to look after the house.

Every project has one named lead who knows your home and answers your calls. It's not complicated, but it makes all the difference.

{# ---------- Craftsmanship ---------- #}

Our approach to quality

How we make sure the work lasts.

  • The right trade

    Each part of the job is done by someone who specialises in it, not whoever happens to be free.

  • Checked as we go

    Your project lead inspects each stage before the next begins, so problems are caught while they're easy to fix.

  • Snagged before we leave

    We walk the finished work with you and fix every small issue before calling the job done.

  • A written record

    You get a handover pack listing materials, finishes and suppliers, for any future repairs.
